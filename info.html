Frequently asked questions

A few helpful answers.

How do I place an order?

Choose a product on the Shop page, complete the order enquiry form and send the prepared WhatsApp message. M&D will confirm availability and final delivery details.

Are the prices final?

Prices currently shown are starter examples and must be confirmed by M&D before launch.

Can I request a customized gift?

Yes. Use the order enquiry form and add your requirements in the notes field.

Is online payment available?

Not yet. This version prepares an order enquiry; live checkout and payment can be added with the future backend.

Shipping & delivery

Delivery areas, charges and estimated timelines will be confirmed by M&D when the order is accepted.

Returns & exchanges

The final return and exchange policy must be supplied and approved by M&D before launch.

Privacy

This static frontend does not save submitted form data. When backend services are introduced, a full privacy notice and secure data handling process must be added.

Terms

Product availability, final prices and order acceptance are confirmed by M&D directly until a live commerce system is implemented.
