How can I place an order?

Choose your favourite product and use the Buy Now or Order Now button to send your order enquiry through WhatsApp.

Can I order honey as a gift?

Absolutely. You can enquire about M&D Honey and ask us about gift-ready presentation and hamper options.

Can I customize a gift hamper?

Yes. Share the occasion and your preferences through our contact or order enquiry form and we'll help you explore options.

How can I ask about delivery?

Send us your city and selected products through WhatsApp so our team can confirm delivery availability and charges.
